# Phase 2 — an organism with a history

Agents now carry personal evidence that changes resource choices, exploration, routes
and willingness to share. Temperament stays stable. Body state still supplies immediate
needs; memory changes what an agent expects from a place, individual or action.

## What you can observe

- An agent returns to places where meals or rest worked, even after losing the immediate
  resource sighting. It searches the remembered location, without knowing whether food
  is still there.
- Attacks make a place less attractive. An agent may use a familiar safer waypoint,
  accept that risk when desperate, or revise avoidance after sustained safe revisits.
- Remembered harm follows a recently observed individual into a different place. Help
  and harm remain separate evidence: someone can have helped and hurt the same agent.
- Sharing depends on kindness, personal experience and accumulated scarcity. Gifts
  remain conserved physical portions. An agent does not learn that somebody refused
  help merely because they were nearby.
- Completed interactions teach expected versus delivered relief and reliability.
  Interrupted meals never teach nutritional success. Deliberately giving away part of
  a meal updates the donor's expectation of its remaining portion.
- In **Inspect Polygon**, the existing decision explanation is followed by memory counts,
  local familiarity, expected danger, scarcity and three recent salient episodes.
  These are summaries of recorded simulation evidence. The panel scrolls and its text
  refreshes at most five times per real second, plus explicit selection changes.

The milestone is automated in `tests/test_history_behavior.gd`: two otherwise identical
worlds choose opposite equivalent food sources after opposite experiences. Swapping
only memories swaps choices; removing memory restores the same choice. Safe
counterexperience can reverse avoidance. JSON restore at a shifted clock preserves
that choice.

## Human qualities and the Maslow reference

The design takes inspiration from Maslow's 1943 discussion of overlapping needs,
exceptions to a rigid priority order, and the effects of a history of gratification
or deprivation. Hunger and caution can coexist; growing urgency changes the balance.
That is a design interpretation, not an implementation of a validated psychological
model. The learning weights and time constants below are game tuning.

Human qualities come from continuity: imperfect recall, stronger direct experience,
lingering expectations after a bad encounter, familiar places, conditional generosity,
and beliefs that can change. Intentions retain the existing hysteresis instead of
switching at every small score fluctuation. There are no hard-coded friend/enemy
labels, private thoughts generated by a language model, or omniscient social facts.

Reference: A. H. Maslow, *A Theory of Human Motivation*, Psychological Review 50(4),
370–396 (1943), particularly “Further characteristics of the basic needs.”
[Original paper transcription](https://www.yorku.ca/pclassic/Maslow/motivation.htm).

## Storage and learning

| Evidence | Maximum per agent | Meaning |
|---|---:|---|
| Place patches | 64 | 2-by-2 world cells; familiarity, food, rest, danger, access |
| Resource observations | 64 | Last visible capabilities and their observed positions |
| Significant individuals | 24 | Familiarity, help, harm, confidence |
| Action expectations | 16 | Local reliability, relief and prediction error |
| Episodes | 32 | Structured salient experiences, including source and time |

Place identity is independent of room IDs, so editing walls does not erase history.
Memory contains IDs, values and observed positions, never mutable world object references.
Old evidence decays lazily with a 900-second time constant. Familiarity and scarcity
sample at most once a simulation second, staggered across agents. Safe visits reduce
place danger. Eight-second gaps distinguish continuous events; completed meals are
separate outcomes. Episodes retain salient recent events rather than an unlimited log.
Witnessed attacks have 45% of direct evidence weight and require visibility of both
participants. The victim attributes harm to its actual attacker, not its own identity.

A resource disappearing or moving while unseen does not update its remembered position.
The agent must inspect the old location or see the new one. Empty-patch expectations
are revised only when the patch can actually be inspected. Individuals' last sightings
expire separately from longer-lived personal evidence.

## Implementation boundaries

`AgentExperienceMemory` owns bounded evidence and eviction. `AgentCognition` interprets
that evidence for the existing decision pipeline. Controllers, providers and combat
remain authoritative for physical effects. Memory never grants food, deals damage or
changes personality directly.

Spatial indices narrow candidates; exact visibility still determines perception.
One immutable perception phase can share geometry and observation calculations, but
each observer receives independent snapshots. The phase ends before sharing, movement,
provider admissions or door changes. Do not retain its caches across simulation ticks.
Use `AgentSimulation.spawn/remove` and `WorldObjectStore.add/move/remove/share_food`
to preserve index and ID-map ownership; do not mutate their public collections directly.

Navigation caches at most 256 routes for two simulated seconds, invalidates on radius
or traversal changes, and reuses current routes. The default population-wide budget is
8,192 A* expansions per tick, with rotating decision priority. The 4,096-expansion per-route
limit also preserves uncertainty when a search is unfinished. `DEFERRED` means an
assessment is pending: no negative evidence, panic setback, inaccessible-food inference
or permission for predation. It does not cap all CPU time: direct geometry queries,
perception and action execution still cost work. Risk sampling uses sixteen points
across a route and tests at most one familiar alternative waypoint.

## Persistence and extensions

`agent.memory.snapshot(now)` returns version-1 JSON-safe data; restore with
`agent.memory.restore(JSON.parse_string(json), new_now)`. Ages are relative, so moving
between simulation clocks does not change evidence strength. Restore validates caps,
fields, finite values, identities and ranges before atomically replacing memory.
These are **cognition snapshots**, not whole-world saves: they do not persist bodies,
resource sightings, intentions, random-generator state or world objects. Keep actor
and object identities stable when restoring into an existing world.

To add an experience, emit structured evidence only from a verified physical outcome
or an observer's perception. Extend the kind/schema, its bounded update rule, the actual
consumer that changes behavior, and inspection wording. Add an outcome-producer test
and a controlled history-swap test. Do not add factual beliefs from whole-world queries.

## Verification and limits

Run `python3 tests/run_tests.py /path/to/godot` for all regression checks and startup.
Run `godot --headless --path . --script tests/benchmark_history.gd` for six hours of
synthetic experience and bounded-memory update timings. Performance measurements and
remaining dense-scene costs are in [simulation-performance.md](simulation-performance.md).

This is rudimentary embodied learning. It does not add dialogue, cultural learning,
gossip, long-term plans, carrying, relationship categories or a full-world save UI.
Route sampling can miss small danger patches, and bounded memories deliberately forget.
